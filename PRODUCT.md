# PRODUCT.md — AdverSim Autonomous Cyber Defense Simulator

## Vision & Purpose
AdverSim is an empirical cyber defense and resilience verification platform designed for enterprise security teams, SOC analysts, and red-team architects. It simulates heterogeneous corporate networks under adaptive adversarial assault (Multi-Armed Bandit UCB1, Markov kill-chain transitions, and Bayesian consensus anomaly detection).

## Target Users
- **SOC Analysts & Incident Responders**: Monitor real-time intrusion telemetry, triage breach paths, and track Mean Time to Detect (MTTD).
- **Security Officers & CISO Teams**: Benchmark architectural resilience across 6 longitudinal ablation conditions (Baseline vs Gossip vs Honeypot Decoys vs Collaborative Consensus).
- **Red Team / Blue Team Architects**: Test network segmentation, Active Directory defenses, and decoy placements against autonomous machine-learning attackers.

## Core Workflows
1. **Explore Architecture & Metrics**: Review theoretical models, live topology radar, and mathematical bandit kernels.
2. **Authenticate & Enforce Zero-Trust**: Log in via Google Account or encrypted email vault, synced with Google Cloud Firebase and Firestore.
3. **Run Live SOC Simulation**: Toggle speeds, inject targeted attacks (Kerberoasting, Phishing, SQLi, Decoy probes), and inspect real-time topology graphs.
4. **Audit Ablation Benchmarks**: Examine empirical MTTD, false-positive curves, and Markov probability matrices.
